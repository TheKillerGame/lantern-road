# The Lantern Road — story outline

## Hook
Long ago the five free peoples lit the **Hearthflame**, a fire that burns in five beacon
towers across the land and keeps the dead in the east asleep. One night every beacon goes out
at once. In the hedge-village of Thornmere the last living ember falls from the sky into a
cracked iron lantern. The lantern will not let anyone else carry it. You leave home with it.

**Goal:** carry the ember along the old Lantern Road and relight the five beacons, so the
peoples answer each other's call again before the **Hollow King** marches out of the Ashfall.

The lantern is also a mechanic. It lights your way at night and is what draws the dead to you.
Its oil gauge is your health, and relighting a beacon upgrades it.

## Regions and bosses
| # | Region | Feel | Town | Boss |
|---|--------|------|------|------|
| 1 | **Thornmere** | Halfling hedge-country, hill homes, mills | Bramblebury | **Old Gnarl**, a rotten willow that walks |
| 2 | **Elderwood** | Silver-barked elven forest, ruins, rivers | Silverhall | **The Weaver**, a spider queen in a fallen shrine |
| 3 | **Khazrim Deeps** | Dwarf mines, forges, lava halls | Stonegate | **Ironwyrm**, a forge-dragon the dwarves woke by digging |
| 4 | **Greyfen** | Haunted marsh, drowned dead, will-o-wisps | none | **The Drowned Herald**, herald of the Hollow King |
| 5 | **The Riven Steppe** | Horse-lord plains, burning watchtowers | none | **Warlord Gharrok** and his siege beast |
| 6 | **The Ashfall** | Volcanic dead land, black towers | none | **The Hollow King** |

You get your horse in Thornmere in act 1. The Steppe is where riding really opens up.

## Companions (choose 2 to travel with)
- **Wren Tallowby** (halfling): thief with a sling. Loyalty quest is finding her brother, who joined a goblin gang.
- **Aelis Vanyr** (elf): archer. Loyalty quest is the dead grove her people abandoned.
- **Borrik Ashhand** (dwarf): hammer and shield, and he unlocks master forging. Loyalty quest is reclaiming his clan's anvil from the Deeps.
- **Maud Fenwick** (human): marsh hedge-witch healer. Loyalty quest is laying her drowned village to rest.
- **Sir Caddock** (human): disgraced knight of the Steppe. Loyalty quest is the twist below.

## Twists
1. **Act 2:** every relit beacon also shows the Hollow King where the flame is. Relighting them is why his army is marching now.
2. **Act 3:** Sir Caddock was the watchman who let the beacons go dark. He was bribed with a promise to save his dying son. You choose whether to forgive him, and the choice changes the finale.
3. **Finale:** the Hollow King was the first lantern-bearer. The flame burned out everything in him except the will to carry it. At the last beacon you choose to keep the ember, which makes you strong but begins the same fate, or to pour it into the beacon and lose the lantern's power for the final fight.

## Endings
- **Pour it, and Caddock was forgiven:** he holds the gate and you win as an ordinary hero. The good ending.
- **Pour it, and Caddock was not forgiven:** you win alone. A bitter ending.
- **Keep the ember:** you beat the King easily, then the epilogue shows you sitting on his throne.
