The Lantern Road

Three looks for the same game. Each one is drawn at 384×216 and scaled up, as the real game would be.

AIlluminated hedge-country

Warm Game Boy Advance palette, Thornmere by day. Health is a row of wax seals, the hotbar is a leather belt, and the quest is lettered on a scroll in pixel blackletter.

BLantern in the dark

A low-colour palette where the lighting does the work, Greyfen at night. The lantern you carry is the health gauge, a candle burns down as stamina, and the moon tells the hour.

CBright sixteen-bit saga

Saturated Super Nintendo colours, Elderwood at noon, riding your horse. Life and spirit are gems set in carved roots, experience grows as a vine, and companions talk through a portrait scroll.
